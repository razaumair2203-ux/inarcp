# Provider metadata attribution

`NR_PreProcessDataForAmpl_June9.m`, `NR_Range_Selection_June9.m`, and
`Sea Clutter Data 09 June 2011.xlsx` are unmodified metadata from M. Ritchie's
[NetRAD monostatic and bistatic sea-clutter dataset](https://doi.org/10.5522/04/32676582),
University College London, 2026, documenting the June 2011 campaign. They retain
the provider's **CC BY-NC 4.0** terms; the repository's MIT software license
does not apply to them. Original filenames and bytes are preserved, with
archive/member verification recorded in `METADATA_AUDIT.json`.

These files establish processing and range-coordinate provenance. They do not
provide the missing complex hardware range response. No raw radar sample array
is redistributed here. `audit_headers.py` is the original audit helper;
`archive_directory.json` records the provider archive listing.

`UW_DATACITE.json` records primary DOI metadata for the UW dataset. UW data
attribution and selective-download terms are stated separately in `../UW_ATTRIBUTION.md`.
