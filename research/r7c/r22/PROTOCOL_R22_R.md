# Part R protocol: new real pedestrians, independent camera association

## Claim and expectations

This is a descriptive real-target check on three fixed public University of Washington radar records. The provider describes labels as synchronized camera object/depth estimates with manual calibration. These labels are external to our detectors, but their depth/timing error and the independence of every manual correction are not quantified. They establish physical association at tolerated spatial/time support, not an exact native-cell return onset. Camera-negative regions are not certified clutter-only ground truth. R21 remains frozen.

Before real FFTs, fits, coherence statistics, calibration scores or detections, root must approve the finalized definitions and create a hash manifest covering this protocol, config, runner, imported methods and acquired source manifest. Only a matching frozen manifest authorizes empirical computation. Outcomes are saved even when a gate fails; no record/bin/timing changes can rescue a failure.

R1: complete raw/camera-label files can be acquired and verified for all three metadata-fixed records. R2: all rotations admit training normalization and enough camera-screened calibration units. R3: aggregated training-only complex AR(1) coefficient has magnitude at least 0.5 in all rotations, a descriptive necessary operating-regime check, not proof of textured-clutter stationarity. R4: test acquisition exceedance on camera-negative backgrounds is between 0.5 and 2 times nominal 0.01 for each method. R5: descriptive association is nonempty in all three test records. No numerical superiority or noninferiority is expected; unknown labels, few independent records and unverified clutter distribution prevent a population claim. Report clean-entry subset emptiness as failure.

## Radar processing and trained quantities

Validate `adcData` complex shape 128 fast-time × 255 chirps × 4 Rx × 2 Tx. Fix Rx 0, Tx 0, chirp 0; use one complex range profile per 30 Hz frame, no channel/dwell picking. Multiply 128 fast samples by symmetric Hann and perform unshifted FFT 128. Physical grid spacing is `c Fs/(2 S N)` with c=3e8, Fs = 4 MHz, S = 21 MHz/us: 0.2232142857 m. No radar peak alignment, target-conditioned phase rotation, zeroing or cross-frame phase repair is applied.

The [authors' FFT code, `range_fft`](https://github.com/Xiangyu-Gao/Radar-multiple-perspective-object-detection/blob/main/slice3d.py) uses unshifted `np.fft.fft(..., axis=0)`. Their [range mapping](https://github.com/Xiangyu-Gao/Radar-multiple-perspective-object-detection/blob/main/utils/mappings.py) and [native MATLAB configuration](https://github.com/Xiangyu-Gao/mmWave-radar-signal-processing-and-microDoppler-classification/blob/main/config/get_params_value.m) map `0..N-1` to positive range as `index*Fs/N*c/(2*S)`, rather than a signed centered FFT grid. The [raw UW converter](https://github.com/Xiangyu-Gao/mmWave-radar-signal-processing-and-microDoppler-classification/blob/main/read_uwcr.m) directly consumes this release's `adcData`; no conjugation/sign flip is applied. These primary source files were saved/hashed in `dataset_evidence/UW_NATIVE_RANGE_SOURCE_LOG.json`. Some provider neural-network code uses FFT 134 with edge cropping; this study deliberately uses the raw release's native FFT 128 and configuration, not that cropped network index.

Preselect 34 consecutive disjoint native groups: [5,6,7], [8,9,10], ... [104,105,106] (zero-based FFT bins). Three native bins per acquisition group; retained centers cover about 1.34..23.44 m. The provider's positive-range convention applies to these indices, including indices above 64. This is an arbitrary uniform field-of-view grid fixed before outcomes, not provider verified per-cell clutter labels.

For each bin use only training frames camera-screened by the complete support of every labelled object, radial margin 1 m, and temporal expansion ±3 frames. Require ≥100 eligible frames and ≥100 eligible consecutive frame pairs per bin; missing CSV is unknown, not negative. Compute per-bin complex mean and residual RMS power from those frames only and apply unchanged to train/cal/test. Fit one aggregated complex AR1 coefficient over normalized, eligible consecutive pairs across all retained bins; clamp magnitude at 0.98. Save unclamped and clamped coefficient, pair counts and per-bin diagnostics. A gate failure applies to that rotation and cannot trigger a better-looking bin subset. Mean removal may remove static returns and is therefore part of the fixed detector definition.

## Acquisition geometry, comparators and calibration

Construct fixed absolute frame episodes starting 2+27j, length 27: a 24-frame prefix and 3 search endpoints. Only complete frame episodes fit in 2..899. Every group/time episode is fixed before labels; missing labels at any required frame invalidate its label classification. The radar scans all retained groups, regardless of camera position. For all methods take the maximum score over identical 3 native bins ×3 endpoints. Calibration of this maximum accounts for that prescribed range/time search; camera labels are used only for masking and evaluation.

At endpoint t use m = 16 samples t-g-16..t-g-1, g = 0 or 8. IN numerator is |x_t-r*x_(t-1)|²; denominator is [(1-|r|²)|H0|² + sum|Hj-r*Hj-1|²]/16, including the stationary first-sample term. CA uses |x_t|² divided by mean raw history power. OS8 uses |x_t|² divided by eighth order raw history power. All share trained normalization and fixed histories.

The persistent comparator is the existing `P-ANMF1-N16` definition in `run_migration.py`: form 16 AR1 innovations e_j=x_j-r*x_(j-1), j=t-15..t, from 17 genuine raw looks; compute `max_k |FFT64(e)_k|² / (16*sum_j |e_j|²)` over k=0..63. Its current-dwell energy normalizes the same scanned Doppler bank, with no camera velocity or oracle phase. A zero-energy innovation dwell scores 0 and is counted separately.  Cross-frame phase coherence is not guaranteed by the provider, so this comparator is descriptive. The 24-look prefix supports it without negative wrapped indices.

Calibration is the designated other record. Use only group episodes whose entire 27 frames are camera-negative under margin 1 m and ±3-frame temporal masking, including every labelled class. Spatial/time units share an episode frame grid and remain dependent; no exchangeability guarantee is claimed. Compute each method's empirical split rank quantile at ceil((n+1)*0.99), requiring n≥100 and finite threshold; strict `score>threshold`. No same-record retuning is performed. Save all calibration scores/counts so rank and multiplicity are reviewable.

Nominal alpha = 0.01 applies to each predefined **three-bin × three-look group episode**. It is not simultaneous false-alarm control over all 34 groups or the full field of view. All groups are scored on a uniform acquisition grid, but calibration and reporting use the group/time unit just defined.

## Independent association and background endpoints

For a labelled rectangle (px±wid/2, py±len/2), its exact radial support is minimum distance from origin to rectangle through maximum corner distance. Expand this support by the frozen margin 0.5 or 1 m. A native bin is occupied when its half-spacing physical interval intersects that support. A fixed 3-bin group has a target association at a search endpoint when any bin is occupied by a labelled pedestrian (class 0). Use all pedestrian track IDs; retain each other class in exclusion masks. Labels outside declared range are retained in audit but associations outside fixed field of view are counted separately, never clipped into a favourable cell.

Report primary contemporaneous presence association per group/episode with margin 0.5 m, and sensitivity margin 1 m. A separate temporal sensitivity uses label frames ±3 around the search endpoints, expanding every target by the same fixed time tolerance; it does not shift signals or choose an optimal offset. All source frames in the expanded interval must have known labels. A hit is any threshold exceedance in the prescribed fixed group/search episode, regardless of camera velocity; this is association recall, not proof the pedestrian caused the alarm.

Secondary clean-entry association requires all prefix frames in the same group to be free of every object's **contemporaneous** margin 1 m support and all relevant CSVs known, followed by contemporaneous pedestrian support in at least one search endpoint. Do not use the ±3-expanded calibration mask for this clean-entry prefix: a target in the search interval would otherwise be projected backward into its own prefix and force this endpoint empty. Record first labelled support-entry frame per track/group for descriptive timing only; entry requires adjacent known label frames with previously absent support, so first/unknown labels do not create entries. The prefix includes 24 looks for the g = 8 comparator. Because an extended body crossing is gradual, this is an external physical entry criterion, not an instantaneous radar-return onset. Do not move windows to camera entry times; report no clean entries if the fixed geometry produces none.

Test background exceedance uses the same camera-negative mask, same 27-frame episodes, same3-bin×3-look maximum, and the test record, disjoint from training and calibration. Require ≥100 such group episodes for the descriptive gate. Report numerator/denominator, rate/0.01, and per-absolute-episode counts. Backgrounds may include unlabeled objects or sidelobes; call them camera-negative backgrounds. Report raw target-associated/background counts alongside rates for every method, margin, timing tolerance, rotation, and track. Also report a matched target-free acquisition floor as the same background exceedance rate.

## Interpretation and reproducibility

Each recording's test rotation is one unit. Report per-record results, their unweighted mean and range, no bootstrap/binomial interval over cell crossings or reused frames. Within-record many association episodes/tracks are dependent descriptive measurements. Three rotations reuse each record in different roles and do not create 9 independent records. Save camera-only classification/entry tables and source provenance, fixed scores/maxima, fits, thresholds, nonfinite/failure logs, frozen hashes and runtime. No absent label, failed phase gate, calibration instability, unknown body-depth error or empty entry subset is described as successful correlated-clutter transient validation.

The runner refuses empirical execution if any prior Part R output or audit file is present in `study/results/r22/R/`. First outcomes and partial audit evidence are preserved; reruns require a separately registered output path/study rather than overwriting them.
